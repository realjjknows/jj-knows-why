The bit to remember
Stars are still shining all day long. Sunlight scattered across our sky is simply brighter than they are.

The Sun is our closest star, and our air scatters its light right across the sky. That glow drowns out every other star, the way a torch beam disappears in daylight. The stars never stop shining — only when the sky goes dark do they come back into view.
